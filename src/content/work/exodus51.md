brief: |
  Exodus51 is a nonprofit that frees slaves in Pakistan while building housing, education, and church around that mission. I report directly to the founder on marketing.

  In 2024 I redesigned and built their Webflow site around a sharp line—“We free slaves”—so visitors could grasp the mission quickly and find a clear path to donate.
problem: |
  The founder-built site informed people and accepted donations, but it lacked the professionalism and brand continuity serious fundraising needs.

  Later, as the org grew, I proposed moving off Webflow toward a fully custom Astro site—owned markup, clearer performance, and room to evolve without template constraints. However, that direction wasn’t adopted.
solution: |
  The Webflow redesign went back to the content: bold mission messaging, sections for what the org does and why them, and unmistakable donate CTAs. That’s the work still featured here.

  Separately I prototyped the Astro redesign on Netlify. The founder preferred another direction and pursued it themselves. The proposal stays linked for reference—not as the live site.
