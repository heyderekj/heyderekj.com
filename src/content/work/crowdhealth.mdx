## Why

October 1 marked two years since my family joined [CrowdHealth](https://joincrowdhealth.com). I use it more than almost any app on my phone, so for the anniversary I gave myself a project: what if I reimagined the brand? This is a concept, made for fun as a member, not work for CrowdHealth.

## The mark

The heart stays. It's what CrowdHealth is about. But instead of a filled heart in a circle, it's drawn as an open, lighter mark, paired with a cleaner wordmark.
