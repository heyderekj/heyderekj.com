description: "Enterprise healthcare marketing site with solution storytelling, stakeholder journeys, and a provider resources microsite—brand refresh and Webflow rebuild with Mast in four weeks, together with Kem Design."
brief: |
  Care Continuity helps patients and health systems close the loop on follow-up care—navigating discharge recommendations, scheduling logistics, and in-network provider alignment. Together with Kem Design, I rethought the site's information architecture and collaborated on page design before building the Webflow experience with Mast.

  Together with Kem Design, I helped evolve the brand to feel more human—replacing dated illustration with real photography, vibrant gradients, and a ribbon motif for the patient–provider connection. I carried that system into the web build for enterprise buyers and into durable templates for solutions and provider resources.
problem: |
  The existing site did not match the credibility Care Continuity needs with hospitals, ACOs, and health plans. Dated visuals and unclear structure made a complex product suite harder to trust and harder to parse.

  Enterprise healthcare buyers evaluate quickly. A product with 100+ health system customers and 3M+ patients navigated deserved a web presence that communicated authority without losing the human side of care navigation.
solution: |
  I reworked the IA around who Care Continuity serves—patients, health systems, and providers—and built modular solution pages for ED Connect, Referral IQ, Readmission IQ, and CarePath IQ so each offering tells a clear story without a one-off layout every time.

  Mast's 12-column component system kept heroes, proof metrics, CTAs, and content bands composable across templates. A separate resources microsite gives providers extensive information without cluttering the main conversion path.

  We shipped the full Webflow build in four weeks—structure, templates, and CMS collections—not a surface-level reskin.
specificWork: |
  - Webflow design and development with Mast (No Code Supply)
  - Information architecture rework: solutions, outcomes, stakeholder journeys, and conversion paths
  - Brand evolution and page design in Figma, together with Kem Design
  - Modular solution page templates (ED Connect, Referral IQ, Readmission IQ, CarePath IQ)
  - Stakeholder storytelling: Patients, Health Systems, and Providers
  - Resources microsite with CMS for provider content
  - Brand system implementation in Webflow: photography, gradients, and ribbon motif across templates
