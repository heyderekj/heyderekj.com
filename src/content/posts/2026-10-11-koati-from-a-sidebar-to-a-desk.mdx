Koati got a new layout this week. The old one was a sidebar: a dark rail down the left with Today, Inbox, Chat, Work and Plan, then every board stacked under them. The new one is a desk. Inbox, Chat and Work sit across the top, the boards you're on are pills under them, and whatever you open is laid out as modules on the desk.

Same app, same demo team, same afternoon. Here's what moved, and why.

## Today and the inbox were the same list

The old Today had a section called To sort. The inbox had one called Needs sorting. They were the same requests drawn twice. So Today retired, and Inbox became everything waiting on your next step: what to sort, what came back from your assistant, what's yours by due date, then what's waiting on somebody else.

## Three modes, not a long list of pages

The sidebar asked you to pick a page first and a board second. The desk flips that. Inbox, Chat and Work are the only three places you go, and the board pills filter all three at once. Plan and Activity moved inside Work. Pinned and busy boards get a pill; everything else is a keystroke away in the one bar at the bottom.

## One next step, pulling

The rule I kept coming back to: one primary action per surface, and focus lands on the next decision. Nobody should have to arrange a layout. The desk remembers your widths, each board's last view, your pins and the shortcuts you use, and there's no setting for any of it.

A board and a card are laid out as their parts, side by side, instead of one long page.
