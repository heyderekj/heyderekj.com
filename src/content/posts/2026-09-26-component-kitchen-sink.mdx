## Working with AI

A single prompt and response, with markdown inside the slots:

<Prompt model="Claude Opus 5.5" tool="Claude Code" date="2026-09-26">
  <div slot="prompt">Images across the site load with a delay. Find out why and fix it without changing how the pages look.</div>
  <div slot="response">
Two causes stack: multi-megabyte PNGs served into 250px slots, and a reveal script that hides `<main>` until JavaScript runs. I'll serve sized variants through the image CDN and stop fading media.
  </div>
</Prompt>

A longer transcript collapses after the first exchange:

<Prompt
  label="Transcript"
  model="Claude Opus 5.5"
  date="2026-09-26"
  caption="Planning this site's writing system."
  turns={[
    { role: 'user', text: 'Find opportunities to make my site more personal.' },
    { role: 'assistant', text: 'A few questions first — how should short posts and links fit alongside your essays?' },
    { role: 'user', text: 'One stream, typed.' },
    { role: 'assistant', text: 'Got it. Essays, notes, and links in one feed, with **status + revision** on essays.' },
  ]}
/>
